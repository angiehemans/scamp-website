### Fixes `shipped`

- **An element pinned to the right edge stays there.** An element placed with `right` and no `left` drew at the left edge on the canvas, and Scamp's next save added `left: 0px`, which moved it in the browser too. It now draws and saves where `right` puts it, and the same goes for `bottom`. Dragging it, or anything placed with `left: 50%`, no longer makes it jump first
- **Adding a variant no longer crashes the canvas.** When a component's root took its frame's width and an element hung past the root's edge, each variant frame grew to reach that element, and Scamp showed **Something went wrong**. Each frame now settles at the size that fits what it draws
- **The Variants section scrolls with the rest of the panel**, instead of staying pinned at the top on a short window
